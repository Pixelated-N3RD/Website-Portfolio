// 03 — Research

Undergraduate thesis

Before moving into software, I spent my final undergraduate year in a wet-lab-adjacent bioinformatics Thesis — my first real experience of using computational Bioinformatics to address a real-world problem.

B.Sc. (Hons.) Thesis · 2023

Identification of Anti-Hepatocellular Carcinoma Candidate Drugs for Drug Repositioning Using Gene Signatures

Submitted to the Department of Zoology, Faculty of Science, at The Maharaja Sayajirao University of Baroda, in partial fulfillment of a B.Sc. (Hons.) in Zoology. The project applied drug-repositioning methodology to hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) — instead of developing a new drug from scratch, it worked from published HCC gene signatures and bioinformatics databases (GEO, KEGG, GO) to computationally flag existing, already-approved drugs as candidates for repurposing against HCC, aiming to cut the years-long cost and risk of conventional drug discovery.

Bioinformatics Gene Expression Analysis Drug Repositioning GEO / KEGG / GO
